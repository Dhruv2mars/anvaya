Anvaya

Privacy Policy · Last updated 11 July 2026

Anvaya (“the App”) is a local-first personal Panchang and daily ratings app.

Data we store

All core data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device: metric names, daily ratings (1–5), optional notes, cached Panchang fields, and coordinates used for Panchang.

We do not require an account, operate a cloud backend for core features in this version, or sell personal data. There is no sync or analytics network call in v1.

Location

If granted, precise coordinates may be used to compute sunrise and Hindu Panchang, cached in settings, and stored on day records. Deny permission to fall back to Delhi. Location is not used for advertising or sharing.

Backup

Android Auto Backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local data.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
